Reflecting on the comprehensive 8-week journey with the First Cloud AI Journey (FCJ), this document outlines my detailed feedback and evaluation of the program.

### Overall Program Evaluation

**1. Working Environment and Intensity**  
The FCJ environment proved to be highly practical and rigorous, offering a significant departure from theoretical academic settings. The substantial workload and strict weekly deadlines fostered strong discipline and time management skills. The active community on platforms like Discord and Zalo provided exceptional support; participants consistently collaborated on troubleshooting, creating a highly effective remote working environment.

**2. Self-Study and Community Support**  
This program placed a strong emphasis on self-directed learning. Navigating the extensive AWS documentation independently to resolve issues, such as CI/CD build failures or permission errors, significantly improved my research capabilities. Instead of relying on direct solutions, I developed the skill to analyze CloudTrail logs, utilize technical forums, and consult official AWS documentation. Furthermore, the collaborative environment among cohort peers—where we actively reviewed and troubleshot each other's errors—established a highly supportive learning culture. The administrative team also demonstrated commendable attentiveness in maintaining schedules and resolving account issues.

**3. Practical Relevance to the Industry**  
While university coursework provided foundational knowledge in networking and operating systems, participating in FCJ translated these concepts into practical skills. Hands-on tasks such as configuring VPCs, Subnets, and Route 53 for the Awsplace project align directly with current industry requirements for Cloud and DevOps engineering roles.

**4. Skills Acquired (Beyond Technical)**  
In addition to technical proficiency in architecting 3-tier applications and utilizing services like EC2, RDS, S3, and Amplify, the program significantly enhanced my problem-solving and technical documentation skills. Approaching system logs methodically to isolate errors has become standard practice. Additionally, compiling weekly technical reports in Markdown has greatly improved my ability to articulate technical processes clearly and professionally.

---

### Addressing the Survey Questions

**What were you most satisfied with during the program?**  
Successfully deploying a web application on a custom domain with an active SSL certificate, coupled with an automated CI/CD pipeline triggered by GitHub repositories, provided a profound sense of professional accomplishment. It effectively demonstrated the ability to architect and deploy a functional real-world system from the ground up.

**What do you think the program should improve for future cohorts?**  
I recommend that the organizers incorporate a detailed instructional session or comprehensive documentation on AWS Billing & Cost Management during the first week. Despite utilizing the AWS Free Tier, misconfigurations can inadvertently lead to unexpected charges. Enhanced warnings regarding cost-incurring services would improve the overall learning experience. Additionally, organizing seminars with industry professionals to share practical insights would be highly beneficial.

**Would you recommend this program to a friend?**  
Absolutely. I have already recommended this program to several peers. For individuals aiming to transition from software development to comprehensive cloud infrastructure operations, FCJ offers a challenging yet highly rewarding environment.

---

### Conclusion

Completing this 8-week program provides a robust foundation for my continuous professional development. I extend my sincere gratitude to the organizing committee and all participating peers for their support throughout this journey. I wish the FCJ program continued success in cultivating highly skilled Cloud engineering professionals.

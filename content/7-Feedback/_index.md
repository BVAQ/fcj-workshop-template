Looking back at the entire 8-week journey with the **First Cloud AI Journey (FCJ)**, I have a lot of thoughts and emotions to share. Below is my most honest and personal feedback regarding the program.

### 🌟 Overall Program Evaluation

**1. Working Environment and Intensity**  
Personally, I found the FCJ environment to be highly "practical and battle-tested." It is completely different from learning theories at university or passively watching online tutorials. The workload and intensity were quite high, with strict weekly deadlines. I was a bit overwhelmed at first, but this pressure forced me to develop strong discipline and time management skills. The community on our channels (Discord/Zalo) was incredibly active; sometimes, even at 1 or 2 AM, if someone asked a question, others would jump in to help or debug together. The feeling of not being alone in this struggle was amazing.

**2. Self-Study and Community Support**  
This program strongly emphasizes self-study, and there were no mentors to spoon-feed us. Initially, it was a huge culture shock having to navigate the massive ocean of AWS documentation completely on my own. However, having to figure things out independently when stuck on a "403 Access Denied" error or a failed CI/CD build drastically improved my research skills. Instead of just asking for a direct answer, I learned how to read CloudTrail, search Stack Overflow, and dig into official AWS Docs. The brightest aspect was the support from peers in the same cohort; we reviewed each other's errors and brainstormed solutions together, creating an environment of self-learning where you never truly felt alone. The Admin team was also incredibly attentive, reminding us of deadlines and resolving account-related issues enthusiastically.

**3. Practical Relevance to the Industry**  
The knowledge I gained at university (like basic networking and operating systems) acted as a foundation. However, joining FCJ and getting my hands dirty configuring VPCs, Public/Private Subnets, and Route 53 turned those dry theories into living, breathing practical skills. The tasks involved in building the Awsplace project align exactly with what the industry currently demands from Cloud/DevOps engineers.

**4. Skills Acquired (Beyond Technical)**  
Of course, technically, I mastered the 3-tier architecture, EC2, RDS, S3, Amplify, etc. But the most valuable things I gained are **troubleshooting skills** and **technical documentation**. I used to hate reading error logs and would panic when I saw a bug. Now, my first reflex is to calmly read the logs, dive into the AWS Docs, and systematically isolate the issue. Writing weekly reports in Markdown has also drastically improved my ability to present technical problems clearly.

---

### 💡 Addressing the Survey Questions

**What were you most satisfied with during the program?**  
The moment I saw my website successfully running on a custom domain with that green HTTPS lock (SSL certificate), and seeing my CI/CD pipeline automatically deploy every time I pushed code to GitHub... The sense of accomplishment was immense. It proved to me that I can actually build a real-world system from scratch.

**What do you think the program should improve for future cohorts?**  
I think the organizers should provide a very detailed guide (or a dedicated session) on **AWS Billing & Cost Management** right in the first week. Even though we use the AWS Free Tier, many students (myself included) sometimes misconfigured or forgot to turn off services, leading to unexpected charges. Having clearer warnings about cost-incurring services would make the experience much smoother. Additionally, having 1-2 online seminars where working Cloud Engineers share their real-world experiences would be highly inspiring.

**Would you recommend this program to a friend?**  
Absolutely, **YES**. I have already recommended it to a few of my university peers. For anyone who wants to transition from just writing code to actually operating a full infrastructure on the Cloud, FCJ is a "tough but incredibly rewarding" environment to experience.

---

### 🎯 Final Thoughts

Finishing these 8 weeks doesn't mean stopping here. The experience at FCJ has provided me with a rock-solid launchpad. I want to send my deepest thanks to the organizing team and especially to all the peers in the community who accompanied and supported each other on this journey. I wish the FCJ community continuous growth, creating many more generations of high-quality Cloud engineers!
